Hi! Welcome to this site. I use it to keep
track of the research I'm working on.

My research relates to the behavioral
theory of the firm. Currently, I am writing
my dissertation on the spaces in which firms
search when they fail to achieve their goals.

Specifically, I am writing about (1) the
direction of problem-solving search, (2)
search by backtracking and branching,
and (3) econometric identification in
performance feedback splines.

I'm joining the academic job market soon.
You can find where I'm presenting these
papers on the [research] tab.
